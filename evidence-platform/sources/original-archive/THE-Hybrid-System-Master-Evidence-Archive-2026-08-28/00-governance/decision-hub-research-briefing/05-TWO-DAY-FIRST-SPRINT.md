# Two-day first research sprint

The first sprint is a map and foundation, not completion of three million lines.

## Day 1 — all-five-engine research map

### Morning

1. Confirm the five research maps and decision questions.
2. Establish source-quality tiers and inclusion/exclusion criteria.
3. Search authoritative guidance and systematic evidence for all five engines.
4. Create a deduplicated source inventory.

### Afternoon

1. Identify the top evidence families per engine.
2. Record major disagreements, limitations, and missing inputs.
3. Draft a small number of atomic claims for every engine.
4. Rank each decision question by impact, harm if wrong, and uncertainty.

## Day 2 — demonstrate evidence compilation

### Morning

1. Deepen the highest-priority Strength question: increase versus maintain after an exercise exposure.
2. Convert verified claims into candidate product policies.
3. Make all non-evidence thresholds explicit.
4. Draft a minimal Strength rule pack and trace every rule backward.

### Afternoon

1. Create representative golden scenarios.
2. Add immutable product-lock validators.
3. Demonstrate a shadow decision receipt.
4. Produce the next research backlog for Recovery, Conditioning, Nutrition, and Coordinator.

## Definition of done

- All five engines have a cited research map.
- A structured source inventory exists.
- Each engine has initial atomic claims, including uncertainties.
- Strength has one end-to-end source-to-rule example.
- No raw source directly controls a decision.
- No claim is presented without a verifiable citation.
- Product policies and evidence findings are visibly separated.
- The remaining three-million-line ambition has a scalable ingestion and review plan.

## Explicitly not done in two days

- exhaustive coverage;
- full-text review of the complete corpus;
- production rule packs for all engines;
- production silent application;
- automated medical or injury decisions;
- a claim that the evidence base is complete.
