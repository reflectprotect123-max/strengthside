# Prompt for the receiving ChatGPT conversation

I am designing a deterministic athlete Decision Hub spanning Strength, Conditioning, Nutrition, Recovery, and Coordinator. Read every file in this ZIP before proposing work.

I want to begin **new, current, web-based research across all five engines**. Browse and cite sources. Prefer primary research, systematic reviews, consensus statements, measurement standards, and authoritative professional guidance. Treat health, injury, illness, and nutrition-related claims as high-stakes.

My long-term ambition is to collect roughly three million lines of evidence, but do not treat line count as the quality metric and do not pretend it can all be validated in two days. Help me build a scalable evidence refinery:

```text
source -> atomic claim -> synthesis -> product policy -> executable rule -> tested decision
```

Runtime decisions must use deterministic rules and validators, not an LLM. Raw corpus text must never directly control athlete behavior. If you use an LLM during research assistance, its extracted claims remain untrusted until checked against the cited source.

Respect all product locks in `01-OWNER-INTENT.md`.

Start with the two-day research sprint in `05-TWO-DAY-FIRST-SPRINT.md`. First show me:

1. your proposed research method;
2. inclusion and exclusion criteria;
3. source-quality grading method;
4. the initial search plan for all five engines;
5. the output structure you will maintain;
6. risks, especially copyright, evidence generalization, contradictory findings, and silent-automation safety.

Then perform the research in visible, reviewable batches. Do not dump an undifferentiated reading list. Organize every source around a decision question and fill the templates in this ZIP. Clearly mark inference, uncertainty, and product-policy choices.
