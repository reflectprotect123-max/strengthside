Sixty-four patients (13.1%) sustained secondary injuries (ipsilateral graft re-rupture or contralateral ACL rupture). Logistic regression achieved the best discriminative performance (AUC = 0.739, 95% CI: 0.672-0.806) and calibration (Brier score = 0.106), although no statistically significant difference was observed between logistic regression and random forest (corrected paired t-test, p = 0.72)
