**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Twelve non-randomized trials were included in the meta-analysis. The injury risk ratio of athletes after functional correction training was 0.39 RR (95 CI [1.50-1.93]; Z = 15.53; P I 2 = 2.6%), indicating an improvement of athletes functional patterns.
