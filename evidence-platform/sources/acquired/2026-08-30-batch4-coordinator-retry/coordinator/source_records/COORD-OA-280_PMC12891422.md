**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
In laboratory testing, the peak isometric quadriceps strength was 7.6% lower in the involved limb (P P P = .003), while the peak hamstring strength was 4.7% higher in the involved limb (P = .017). All biomechanical measures demonstrated impaired loading of the injured limb with interlimb differences ranging from 6.6% to 67% (P < .005).
