**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Sixteen studies comprising 1,147 female athletes were included. The pooled RTS rate post-ACLR was 69% (95% CI 63-75%). RTS rates were higher among those returning to professional-level competition (78%) compared to general sports participation (63%) (p = 0.020). Meta-regression indicated an inverse association between RTS time and RTS rates (β = - 0.147, p = 0.046). The average time to RTS was 10.
