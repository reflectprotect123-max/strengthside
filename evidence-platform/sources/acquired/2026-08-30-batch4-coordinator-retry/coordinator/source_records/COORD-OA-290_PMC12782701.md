**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The mean age of the cohort was 24 years. The fifth metacarpal was the most commonly affected site, and falling onto the pitch was the predominant injury mechanism. Mean return to training was 3.16 weeks, to competition 4.12 weeks, and to radiographic union 4.88 weeks. Mean DASH scores improved from 67.5 to 12.8, VAS from 5.78 to 0.75, MCP joint ROM from 66.75° to 89.25°, and grip strength from 44.
