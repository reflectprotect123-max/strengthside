**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The review analysed various types of ankle fractures, with an average patient age of 31.4 years. ORIF was the predominant treatment (89.4%), and the average return-to-sport rate was 87.1%. Time to return varied, with an average of 134.5 days. Recovery time was longer for more severe fractures, such as bimalleolar and trimalleolar fractures, which showed times of up to 720 days.
