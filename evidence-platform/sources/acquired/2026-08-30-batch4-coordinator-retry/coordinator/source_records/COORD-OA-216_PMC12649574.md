**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Rehabilitation after anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction cannot be reduced to a linear, time-based sequence of protection, strength, and return to sport. Persistent asymmetries, quadriceps inhibition, and variable re-injury rates highlight that recovery is a complex adaptive process in which outcomes emerge from dynamic interactions between biological, neural, and psychological subsystems.
