**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Fourteen studies comprising 901 elite athletes were included. The pooled RTS rate following ankle syndesmosis injuries was 96% (95% CI 93-98%), with low-to-moderate heterogeneity (I2 = 27%). Subgroup analysis showed comparable RTS rates for suture-button fixation (98%) and nonoperative management (98%). Sensitivity analyses confirmed the robustness of the findings.
