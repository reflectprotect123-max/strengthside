**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
A total of 86 athletes (43 per group) completed a minimum 5-year follow-up. Both groups showed significant postoperative improvements in all functional scores. At final follow-up, no between-group differences were observed (KOOS QoL mean difference 2.1; Kujala 0.8; IKDC 1.2; Lysholm 1.5; all p 0.05). MCID and PASS achievement rates were comparable between groups. Recurrence occurred in one patient
