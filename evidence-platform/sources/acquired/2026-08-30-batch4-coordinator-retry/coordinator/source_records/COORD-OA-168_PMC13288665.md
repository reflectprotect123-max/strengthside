**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Across all target audiences, GPT-4 outperformed JEPs in both quality and adaptiveness of responses (p < 0.001). For responses aimed at patients, GPT-4 was rated best in 26 (55%) questions. For responses aimed at physical therapists, GPT-4 was rated best in 34 (64%) questions. Performance varied by topic, but GPT-4 consistently provided more expert-adapted and contextually appropriate information.
