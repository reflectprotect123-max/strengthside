**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
A total of 47 patients (47 elbow joints; mean age, 40.7 ± 13 years; 22 men) were analyzed, with a median follow-up of 77 months (interquartile range [IQR], 36-110). The EI-RSI demonstrated good construct validity, showing a moderate positive correlation with MEPS (r = 0.472; P r = -0.572; P r = -0.610; P r = -0.595; P P P = .017). No floor or ceiling effects were observed.
