**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Both hip rotation ROM and trunk endurance improved significantly over time in both groups (p .05). Lead hip ER muscle strength showed a significant interaction, with greater values in the GLEP group (p = .017). There was no significant difference in golf performance measures between groups (p > .05).
