**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
A total of 1011 ACL injuries were identified in 862 unique players; 15.5% (n = 134) sustained ≥2 ACL injuries. Mean RTP duration increased over time (196.7 ± 50.9 days in 2005-2010 vs. 219.9 ± 67.4 days in 2015-2020; p p = 0.031). Post-ACL match exposure was strongly associated with reinjury risk (OR: 1.39 per 1000 min, p p a > 2-fold higher reinjury risk compared with 2005-2010 (p p = 0.034).
