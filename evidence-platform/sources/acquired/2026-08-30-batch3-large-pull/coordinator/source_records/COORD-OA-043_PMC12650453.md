**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The adoption of wearable sensors for precision training has accelerated in recent years, yet most studies and reviews remain device- or feasibility-centric and lack a field-ready decision framework. This review organizes wearable sensing across four monitoring dimensions-physiological, kinematic, biochemical, and dynamic-and maps them onto three training pillars: physical, technical, and tactical.
