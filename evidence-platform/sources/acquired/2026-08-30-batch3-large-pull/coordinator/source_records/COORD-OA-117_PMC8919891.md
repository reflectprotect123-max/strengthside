**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Quantifying external load during futsal competition can provide objective data for the management of the athlete's performance and late-stage rehabilitation. This study aimed to report the match external load collected via wearable technology according to time periods (i.e., halves) and contextual factors (i.e., team's ranking, match result, and location) in elite futsal.
