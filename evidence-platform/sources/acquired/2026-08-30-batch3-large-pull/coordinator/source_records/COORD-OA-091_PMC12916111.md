**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Thirty RCTs were included, and the overall quality was high. The meta-analysis showed that CWI significantly reduced post-exercise CK levels (g = -0.24; 95% CI: -0.37 to -0.10, P P P > 0.05) or MVIC (g = 0.08; 95% CI: -0.08 to 0.23, P > 0.05). Subgroup analysis indicated no significant difference between whole-body and partial immersion at any follow-up time point (0-72 h) (between-group P > 0.05)
