**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
No group differences were seen for CK, MDA, or TAC (p > 0.05). Significant group × time interactions were observed for CAT (p p = 0.031), and SOD (p = 0.025), indicating greater enzyme increases in HMB-FA (CAT + 54%, GPX + 38%, and SOD + 16%) than placebo (+7-14%). LDH declined in both groups (interaction, p = 0.001), but more so in the placebo group (-29.9%) than in the HMB-FA group (-18.6%).
