Endurance training camps are well established in elite sports, but one-week camps for recreational endurance athletes have recently gained popularity despite limited scientific evidence. This study investigated the effects of a one-week endurance training camp on body composition, endurance performance, and markers of metabolic stress and mitochondrial adaptation in recreational athletes.
