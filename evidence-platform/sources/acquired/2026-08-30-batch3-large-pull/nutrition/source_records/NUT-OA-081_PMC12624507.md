**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
A total of 20 randomized controlled trials involving 329 participants were included, evaluating eight dietary interventions. Moderate-quality evidence indicated that carbohydrate supplementation significantly improved the percentage of maximal oxygen uptake (VO2max) compared to placebo (SMD = 1.13, 95% CrI: 0.25 to 2.05) and reduced RPE scores (MD = -0.77, 95% CrI: -1.83 to -0.09).
