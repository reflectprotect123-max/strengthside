**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Resistance exercise was positively associated with intake of protein, vitamins B2, B3, B5, B6, and B12 and the percentage of total calories from protein (PCT-PRO), and negatively associated with the percentage of total calories from carbohydrate (PCT-CHO) (p p p p p < 0.05).
