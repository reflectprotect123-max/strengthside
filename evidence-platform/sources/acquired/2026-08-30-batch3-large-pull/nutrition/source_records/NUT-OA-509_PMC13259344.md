**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
ID athletes had significantly lower fiber, iron, folate, and vitamin C intakes than non-ID. They also showed significantly lower HGB, HTC, MCV, MCH, and ferritin, and higher transferrin, UIBC and TIBC. Iron intake correlated positively with HGB, HTC, MCV, MCH, serum iron, and ferritin, and negatively with UIBC.
