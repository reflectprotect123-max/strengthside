**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The study participants comprised 1154 physically active women (age = 28.5 ± 7.7 years; height = 165.2 ± 6.2 cm; body weight = 60.5 ± 9.7), representing 78 recreational and competitive level sports. The results showed that 40.0% (n = 462) were considered at risk for the Triad, while 24.3% (n = 280) were at risk for subclinical eating disorder, and 7.3% (n = 84) at risk for clinical eating disorder.
