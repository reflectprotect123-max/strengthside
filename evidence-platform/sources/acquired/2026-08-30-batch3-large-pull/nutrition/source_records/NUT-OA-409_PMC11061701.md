**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The prevalence of low BMD (Z-score P = 0.04), total hip (-1.1 ± 1.2 vs 0.0 ± 1.1; P P < 0.01). At the lumbar spine, low BMD was completely absent in wheelchair basketball and tennis players. Regression analyses identified body mass, wheelchair dependence, and type of sport, as the main risk factors for low BMD.
