**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
This systematic literature review aimed to determine the protein requirements of pre-menopausal (e.g., 18-45 years) female athletes and identify if the menstrual cycle phase and/or hormonal contraceptive use influence protein requirements.
