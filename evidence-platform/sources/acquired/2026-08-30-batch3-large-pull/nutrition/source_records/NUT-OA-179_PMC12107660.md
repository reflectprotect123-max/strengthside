**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
In total, 36 studies with 1564 participants were included in the systematic review, with only 31 studies included in the meta-analysis due to missing data. For studies lasting at least 10 weeks, AT outperformed RT in reducing body mass (mean difference (MD) = -1.82 kg [95% CI = -2.72 to -0.93]; p p = 0.01) but led to less FFM retention (MD = - 0.88 kg [95% CI = -1.73 to -0.03], p = 0.04).
