**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Seven athletes completed the survey with the average weight loss during competition preparation being 6.4 kg. 42.9% of respondents reported using weight-cutting supplements and 28.6% reported using anabolic steroids. 85.7% of competitors had regular periods before competition and 57.1% experienced amenorrhea for at least one menstrual cycle during competition preparation.
