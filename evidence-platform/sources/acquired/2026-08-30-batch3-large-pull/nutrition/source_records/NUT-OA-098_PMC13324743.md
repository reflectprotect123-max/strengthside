**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Women experience musculoskeletal changes following pregnancy that may increase musculoskeletal injury risk. This trial explored 18-weeks combined rehabilitation and endurance and resistance training (Intervention, n = 17) compared with standard-of-care (Control, n = 14) on areal bone mineral density (aBMD), tibial bone, and patella tendon properties in postpartum UK Servicewomen.
