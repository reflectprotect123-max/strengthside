**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Despite significant research efforts to advance the field, the mechanisms underlying the association between handgrip strength (HGS) and morbidity and/or mortality still need to be fully understood. We focused on the debate in previous studies regarding the mechanism of this association and examined the impact of the main covariates utilized in those studies on HGS and morbidity and/or mortality.
