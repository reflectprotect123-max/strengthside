Celiac disease is an autoimmune disorder that compromises the integrity of intestinal mucosa and impaired nutrient absorption. Gluten-free diet is currently the only available therapy, but it is not always sufficient to ensure adequate nutritional intake and may be associated with micro- and macronutrient deficiencies, with potential metabolic and functional consequences, particularly in athletes
