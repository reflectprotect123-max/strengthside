**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Bivariate analyses found no correlations between total dietary protein intake and pQCT-derived bone and muscle measures. However, protein intake from animal products was correlated with SSIp at the 38% (r = 0.39, P = 0.008) and 66% site (r = 0.44, P = 0.002), cortical vBMD (r = -0.34, P = 0.02) at the 66% site, and calf muscle CSA (r = 0.57, P P = 0.009).
