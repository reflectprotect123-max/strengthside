**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Cortisol increased post-exercise in both groups and remained elevated at 24 h, with prolonged elevation in the High anxiety group (+17.9% vs. +7.8%; p = 0.03). Testosterone peaked at 1 h, with a larger rise in the High anxiety group (+42.2% vs. +31.5%; p = 0.02). β-endorphin increased post-exercise in both groups (p p < 0.05). Performance time during the 2000-m test was comparable between groups.
