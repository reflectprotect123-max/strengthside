**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
HRVT2 showed moderate-to-strong associations with VT2 for VO2 (rho = 0.88, ICC = 0.86), for HR (r = 0.88, ICC = 0.81) and for PO (r = 0.82, ICC = 0.85). Mean biases were small and limits of agreement (LoA) narrow. HRVT1 correlated only modestly with VT1 for VO2 (rho = 0.67, ICC = 0.43) and weakly for HR (r = 0.43, ICC = 0.37) and PO (r = 0.49, ICC = 0.35), with wide LoA.
