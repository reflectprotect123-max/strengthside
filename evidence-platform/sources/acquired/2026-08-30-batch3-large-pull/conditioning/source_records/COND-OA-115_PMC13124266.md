**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Attempts to make a sharp distinction between ‘true’ recovery and compensation create ambiguity about the nature of motor improvement in neurological rehabilitation. This paper proposes a new framework to resolve this dichotomy: a stratified model where recovery is not a binary concept but is instead defined by its depth across the motor system’s multiple layers, from neurons to task performance.
