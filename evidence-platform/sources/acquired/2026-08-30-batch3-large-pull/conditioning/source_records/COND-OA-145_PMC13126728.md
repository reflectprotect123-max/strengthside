**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The training period coincided with significant shifts in circulating immune markers. Total leukocyte counts and serum IgG levels were significantly lower post-training (P P P P < 0.001). The ABM simulation qualitatively reproduced these emergent patterns, visualizing the non-linear contraction of T-cell populations consistent with the empirical data.
