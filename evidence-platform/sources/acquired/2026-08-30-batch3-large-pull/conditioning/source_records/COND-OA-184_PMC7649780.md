**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The aim was to investigate the effect of training, sex, age and selected genes on physiological and performance variables and adaptations before, and during 6 months of training in well-trained cross-country skiers. National-level cross-country skiers were recruited for a 6 months observational study (pre - post 1 - post 2 test). All participants were tested in an outside double poling time trial
