**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The results showed that the cognitive state of DE model mice was improved and neuronal injury was ameliorated after RC administration. Active compounds in RC, berberine and coptisine, were found to ameliorate HT22 injury induced by high glucose. Network pharmacology results suggest that voltage-gated sodium channel subtypes (Nav1.1, Nav1.2, and Nav1.6) may be the targets for RC prevention of DE. F
