**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Ferroptosis, a regulated form of cell death driven by iron-dependent lipid peroxidation, has emerged as a critical link between cellular senescence and Alzheimer's disease (AD). Senescent cells disrupt iron metabolism, promote peroxidation-prone lipid remodeling, and suppress antioxidant defenses, creating a pro-ferroptotic environment that accelerates neuronal degeneration.
