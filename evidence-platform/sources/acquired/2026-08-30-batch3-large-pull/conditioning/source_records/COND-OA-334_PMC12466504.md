**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The findings indicated a significant decrease in COD time (p 2p = 0.682) and 30-15 IFT (p 2p = 0.735) in the experimental group. However, no statistically significant change was observed in these parameters between the pre-test and post-test in the control group. Additionally, group-time interaction effects were found to be significant in favor of the experimental group in all parameters.
