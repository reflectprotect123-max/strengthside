**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The results indicated that the participants used a similar parabolic reversed-J shaped strategy, with significant 5000-m time improvements observed in both the HIIT1220m (Δ: 20.1 ± 23.7 s, p = 0.007) and the HIIT2850m (Δ: 16.7 ± 25.9 s, p = 0.003) groups. A significant main effect of time (except at the 2nd km, p = 0.693) and a main effect of group (at the 1st km, p = 0.034 and 5th km, p = 0.044)
