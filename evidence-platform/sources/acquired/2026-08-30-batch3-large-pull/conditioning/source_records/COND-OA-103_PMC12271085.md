**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Cardiovascular drift and aerobic decoupling demonstrated a significant strong linear correlation. All ML models achieved good predictive performance in classifying athletes as responders or non-responders, with cross-validation accuracy ranging from 0.87 to 0.9. Average predictive accuracy of 0.86 was for k-nearest neighbors, 0.91 for logistic regression, 0.93 for Variational Gaussian Process mode
