**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
A total of 23 randomized cross-over trials involving 1,146 participants were included. The results showed that protein supplementation during endurance training led to a small, nonsignificant increase in lean body mass (SMD = 0.13, 95% CI: -0.01, 0.28; p = 0.07) and a significant improvement in time to exhaustion (TTE) during endurance exercise (SMD = 0.45, 95% CI: 0.15, 0.76; p < 0.01).
