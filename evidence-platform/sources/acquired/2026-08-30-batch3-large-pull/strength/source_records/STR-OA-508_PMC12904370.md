**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The aim of the study was to evaluate the health-related effects of a 9-month walking-football (WF) season in already active older adults, comparing participants aged 50-59 and ≥60. The study employed a longitudinal pre-post design conducted in a real-world community setting. The participants were 32 adults aged over 50 (including 2 women) who completed a 9-month, twice-weekly outdoor WF program. B
