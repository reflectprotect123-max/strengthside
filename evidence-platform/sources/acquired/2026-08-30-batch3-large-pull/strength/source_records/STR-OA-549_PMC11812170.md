**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Resistance training has been shown to be a stressor factor on the autonomic nervous system, and these changes can be detected by heart rate variability (HRV) analysis. The aim of the present study was to evaluate the delayed effects of velocity loss-based resistance training strategies on heart rate variability (HRV), the sleep quality index (SQI) and delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS).
