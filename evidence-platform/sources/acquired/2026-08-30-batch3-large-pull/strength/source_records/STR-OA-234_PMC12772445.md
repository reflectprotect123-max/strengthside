**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
17 RCTs (n =739) met the inclusion criteria. Pooled analyses showed that resistance training significantly improved overall cognitive function (SMD = 0.40, P 0.05). Heterogeneity and publication bias analyses indicated stable and unbiased results.
