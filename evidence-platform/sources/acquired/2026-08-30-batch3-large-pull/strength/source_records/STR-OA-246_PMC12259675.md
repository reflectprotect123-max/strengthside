**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Tapering did not affect any of the lower body physical and morphological measures. Training cessation induced declines in braking rate of force development (13.1%, p p p = 0.03). All metrics returned to post-taper values within 1 week of retraining.
