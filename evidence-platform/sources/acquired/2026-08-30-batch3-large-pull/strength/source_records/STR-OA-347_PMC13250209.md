**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Sixteen studies were eligible (n = 801), with data available from fifteen moderate quality randomized controlled trials. Compared to control, resistance training was effective in improving muscle mass (g = 0.16, I 2 = 67%), muscle strength (g = 0.55, I 2 = 11%), and functional performance (g = 0.76, I 2 = 62%). Muscular fitness was significantly moderated by load intensity, frequency, and movement
