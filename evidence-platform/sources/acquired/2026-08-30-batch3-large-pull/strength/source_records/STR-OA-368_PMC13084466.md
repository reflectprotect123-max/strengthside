**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The results demonstrate that HBCT led to a notable enhancement in the majority of cardiometabolic health indicators (e.g., anthropometrics, blood pressure, and glucose metabolism), KOA parameters related to quality of life and functionality, and cardiorespiratory fitness when compared with CON (p < 0.05). No significant changes were observed in total bilirubin, sodium, urea, and resting heart rate
