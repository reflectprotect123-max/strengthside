**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Resistance training is a structured exercise modality aimed at enhancing strength, hypertrophy, and overall fitness. However, it carries an inherent risk of musculoskeletal injuries, particularly in the shoulder, lower back, knee, and wrist/hand. The most common injury mechanisms include muscle strains, tendon tears, tendinitis, and sprains, with acute injuries predominating. Key contributing fact
