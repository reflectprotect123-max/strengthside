**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Findings demonstrated significantly greater improvements in selected performance and biomarker outcomes in the WB-EMS group compared to other groups. Performance tests revealed significant enhancements in sit-ups (η p 2 = 0.35), push-ups (η p 2 = 0.54), and strength measures (η p 2 = 0.46) compared to RT and control groups. Biomarker analysis showed WB-EMS induced a 15.88% increase in IL-15 vs. 8.
