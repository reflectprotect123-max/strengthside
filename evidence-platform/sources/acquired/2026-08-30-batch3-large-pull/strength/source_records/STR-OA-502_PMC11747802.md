**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Out of 271 identified papers, 16 studies of moderate to high quality were included in the meta-analysis. Resistance training demonstrated a small but significant effect on serve speed (ES = 0.53; p p = 0.001). However, core training did not have a significant effect on serve speed (ES = 0.32, p = 0.231).
