**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
In total, 27 participants completed the program (IN: n=15 IN; GR: n=12). IN training resulted in better eccentric power gains compared to GR training when assessed using the IN system, although the difference was only statistically significant for the side lunge. For forward lunge, between-group difference was 4.50 W (95% CI -2.94 to 11.94 W, P=.23); for side lunge, between-group difference was 9.
