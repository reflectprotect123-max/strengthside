**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Exercise adaptation depends on a dynamic alternation between catabolic and anabolic states coordinated primarily by AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) and mechanistic target of rapamycin (mTOR). While transient activation of these pathways underpins beneficial molecular remodeling, the system-level consequences of sustained anabolic drive remain insufficiently conceptualized in exercise biology.
