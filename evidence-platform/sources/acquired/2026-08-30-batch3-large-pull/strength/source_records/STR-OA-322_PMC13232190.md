**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
This case report illustrates the feasibility of incorporating early-phase BFR within a criterion-based rehabilitation program following Bankart repair in a collision sport athlete with a Buford complex. Further investigation is warranted regarding standardized upper extremity BFR protocols and return-to-sport testing strategies for contact athletes.
