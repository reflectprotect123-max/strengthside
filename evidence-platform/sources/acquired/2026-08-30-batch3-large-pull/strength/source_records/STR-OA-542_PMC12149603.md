**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The purpose of this study was to assess the effects of a 6-week isometric training intervention on time of flight (ToF) in elite youth level gymnasts. Fourteen nationally elite youth gymnasts (10 females and 4 males; age = 15 ± 2 years; mass = 55.0 ± 8.2 kg and stature = 163.7 ± 6.5 cm) were recruited.
