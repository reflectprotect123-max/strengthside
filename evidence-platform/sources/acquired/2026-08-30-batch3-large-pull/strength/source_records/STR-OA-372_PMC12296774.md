Overall, 43 studies with 1660 participants across 72 different RT interventions were eligible for inclusion. The overall effect on dynamic strength was significant and moderate magnitude (SMD = 0.98, 95% confidence interval 0.91-1.06, p < 0.001), whereas the transfer to non-trained isometric strength measures was significant but small (SMD = 0.42, 95% confidence interval 0.35-0.49, p < 0.001).
