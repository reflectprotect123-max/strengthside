**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Postural instability and impairments in dynamic balance are common in aging populations and are often linked to asymmetries between the supportive and non-supportive limbs. This study investigated the effects of a four-week unilateral posterior-chain resistance training program targeting the non-supportive leg on dynamic and static balance measures in middle-aged, recreationally active adults.
