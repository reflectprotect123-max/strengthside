**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Exercise training can improve mortality, quality of life, exercise capacity and morbidity in cancer patients. Although current cardio-oncological guidelines recommend exercise intervention in cancer patients, a structured, interdisciplinary approach from diagnosis to maintenance of therapy including repetitive sports cardiological assessment to guide exercise intervention has not been established.
