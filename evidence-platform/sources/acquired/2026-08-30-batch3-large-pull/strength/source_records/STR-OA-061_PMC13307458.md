**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The bowling performance of cricket fast bowlers is highly dependent on lower limb power and stiffness. French Contrast Training (FCT) and Velocity-Based Training (VBT) are effective ways to improve rate of force development and peak power. The objective of this study was to investigate the effects of VBT-optimized FCT on the lower limb explosive power and bowling performance of cricket fast bowler
