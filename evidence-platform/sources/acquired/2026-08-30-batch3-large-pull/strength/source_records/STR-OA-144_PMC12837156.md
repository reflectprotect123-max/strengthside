**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
All four interventions were associated with improvements in at least one respiratory or cardiopulmonary domain. FVC and FEV1 tended to improve more in the resistance-combination groups, whereas the FEV1/FVC ratio increased with RMT alone and when combined with resistance.
