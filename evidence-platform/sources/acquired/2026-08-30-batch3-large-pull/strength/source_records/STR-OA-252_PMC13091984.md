The ED and EMD groups showed increases in High Frequency (HF), Root Mean Square of Successive RR Interval Differences (RMSSD), Standard Deviation of NN Intervals (SDNN), Very-Low-Frequency (VLF), Low-Frequency (LF), and VO2peak. There was a decrease in resting Heart Rate (HR), LF/HF, and HbA1c levels. The MD group had a substantial increase in HF and RMSSD and a significant decrease in HR and LF/H
