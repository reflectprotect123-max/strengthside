**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The results showed that Wingate testing and interval training led to decreased sleep duration, increased duration of stress, decreased RMSSD, and increased LF/HF ratio (p p > 0.05). The impact of interval training on sleep parameters was more significant compared to aerobic capacity testing (p p < 0.01).
