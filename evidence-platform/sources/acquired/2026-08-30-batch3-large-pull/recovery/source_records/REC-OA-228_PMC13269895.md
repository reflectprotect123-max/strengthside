**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Professional futsal players experience significant muscle injury, inflammation, and body composition alterations due to intense training. This 12-week double-blind, randomized clinical trial investigated the efficacy of synbiotic supplementation in 25 players assigned to either a synbiotic group (n = 13) receiving 10⁹ CFU of seven probiotic strains plus 375 mg fructooligosaccharides, or a placebo
