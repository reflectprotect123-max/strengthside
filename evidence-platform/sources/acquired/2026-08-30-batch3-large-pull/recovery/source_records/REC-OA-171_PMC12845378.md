**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
FODMAP consumption increases the risk of intestinal symptoms. Short-term FODMAP restriction, especially before and during exercise, reduced the severity of symptoms in most of the analyzed studies. Data on the long-term effects of a low FODMAP diet on the health, nutrition, and gut microbiota of athletes remain limited.
