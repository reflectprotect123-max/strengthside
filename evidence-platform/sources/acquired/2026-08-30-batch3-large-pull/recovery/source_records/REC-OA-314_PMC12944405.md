**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Training load monitoring in women's volleyball is a challenge for optimizing performance and mitigating injury risk. Non-invasive monitoring technologies and machine learning (ML) can support decision-making, but the evidence remains heterogeneous. This scoping review mapped and integrated the evidence on training load management, fatigue, and performance in women's volleyball and identified gaps.
