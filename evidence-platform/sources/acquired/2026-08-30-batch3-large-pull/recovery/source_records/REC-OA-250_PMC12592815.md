**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Sleep monitoring is a tool widely used to support recovery and performance in endurance athletes. This study aimed to assess agreement between research-grade actigraphy (ActiGraph GT9X), consumer-grade smartwatches (Garmin), and self-reported sleep diaries in masters endurance athletes.
