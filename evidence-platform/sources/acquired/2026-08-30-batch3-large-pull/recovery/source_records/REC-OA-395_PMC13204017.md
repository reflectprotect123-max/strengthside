**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Groups did not differ at baseline (p > 0.05). The VOM group improved significantly across all EI dimensions and performance (p ≤ 0.005, η2p ≥ 0.176), whereas the control group showed statistically significant declines in most EI variables (p ≤ 0.019, d ≥ 0.66). Between-group differences were significant for all outcomes, with large effect sizes (d = 0.90-3.08, 95% CI [0.32, 3.85]). Significant Gro
