**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Of 8,677 studies, 97 studies were included. Most research covered orthopaedics (70.1%) and neurology (18.6%), where AI was applied for injury prediction, diagnostic image analysis, and recovery estimation. Predictive and estimation models were the dominant application (57.7%). Reported discriminative performance was frequently high. However, the majority of studies relied on retrospective datasets
