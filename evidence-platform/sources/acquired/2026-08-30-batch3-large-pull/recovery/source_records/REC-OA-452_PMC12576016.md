The aim of this study was to evaluate the effects of daily partial body cryostimulation exposures on sleep and recovery parameters in elite swimmers undergoing an intense training period. Twenty-three elite French swimmers (7 females and 16 males) were involved in this controlled cross-over protocol. The experiment took place during 2 weeks of intense training load.
