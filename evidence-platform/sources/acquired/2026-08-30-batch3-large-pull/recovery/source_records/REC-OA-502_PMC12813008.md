**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Psychological strain significantly predicted resilience, and coping humor showed a direct positive effect on resilience. Significantly, coping humor moderated the relationship between strain and resilience, such that the positive association was strongest at high levels of coping humor.
