**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Paediatric athletes are not simply 'mini adults'. Most existing recommendations for cardiac screening in paediatric athletes are primarily based on evidence in adults and are designed for adult athletes. Paediatric-specific recommendations are needed due to the specifics of cardiac physiology, maturation and growth, age-related disease expression, modified diagnostic pathways, training adaptations
