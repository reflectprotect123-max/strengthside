**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The statistical significance level was set at α = 0.05 for all analyses. Compared to pre-cTBS, the cTBS group demonstrated significant reductions in sleep onset latency (SOL) (P P P P P P P < 0.01). In contrast, no significant differences were observed between groups in standing long jump performance, reaction speed, daytime sleepiness scores, or positive and negative affect scores.
