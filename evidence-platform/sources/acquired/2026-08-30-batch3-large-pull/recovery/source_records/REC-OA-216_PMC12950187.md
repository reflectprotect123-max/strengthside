**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
This umbrella analysis incorporated five meta-analyses (encompassing 69 RCTs) and one additional recent RCT, totaling 3,413 participants. Results showed that probiotic supplementation significantly reduced levels of the pro-inflammatory marker TNF-α (Effect Size (ES) = -0.59, 95% Confidence Interval (CI) [-0.94 to -0.24], P = 0.001).
