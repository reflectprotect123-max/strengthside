**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Significant decreases in jump height (p = 0.010) and average propulsive power (p = 0.005) were observed in the 45% BW condition at 3 and 9 min. Jump momentum decreased significantly at 30% and 45% BW at 3 and 9 min (p = 0.010; p = 0.033). No significant differences were detected in other CMJ force-time metrics (p > 0.05).
