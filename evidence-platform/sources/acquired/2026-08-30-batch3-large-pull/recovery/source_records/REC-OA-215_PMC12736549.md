**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
This study examined the effect of competition stress on recovery time in female collegiate soccer players. Thirty NCAA Division I athletes were monitored over 35 consecutive days using Polar Team Pro wearable devices, which captured exercise duration, distance covered, energy expenditure, sprint count, speed, heart rate, training load, and recovery duration. Data were collected across 20 practices
