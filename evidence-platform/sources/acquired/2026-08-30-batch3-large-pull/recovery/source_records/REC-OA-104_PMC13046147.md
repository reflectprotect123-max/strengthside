**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
Swimmers waking fatigued was positively associated with frequency (p < 0.001) and severity (p < 0.001) of URS, plus negatively associated with salivary IgA (p = 0.035). Perception of meeting 7-9 hour national sleep recommendations was positively associated with URS frequency (p < 0.001) and severity (p = 0.001).
