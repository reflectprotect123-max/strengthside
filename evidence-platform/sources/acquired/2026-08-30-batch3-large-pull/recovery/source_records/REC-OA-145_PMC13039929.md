**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The athlete disability index (ADI) is a self-administered scale developed specifically for athletes to assess functional disability due to low back pain. This study aimed to translate and adapt the ADI into Turkish, examine its validity and reliability, and to evaluate its clinical applicability in athletes with non-specific low back pain (NSLBP).
