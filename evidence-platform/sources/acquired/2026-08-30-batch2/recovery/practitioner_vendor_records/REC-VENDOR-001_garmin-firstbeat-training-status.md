**Evidence note (quoted/paraphrased from the fetched page):**
Training status is computed from three inputs: (1) Acute Training Load - exercise load from heart-rate-monitored activities, decaying over 10 days; (2) VO2 max - estimated via heart rate and performance analytics rather than lab testing; (3) Heart Rate Variability - captured only during sleep, on compatible devices. The feature "interprets changes in your fitness (VO2 max) relative to trends in your training volume and composition (acute training load)." Garmin's own page notes the device shows "no status" when data is insufficient, and HRV-based assessment is incomplete for users who don't wear the device overnight.
