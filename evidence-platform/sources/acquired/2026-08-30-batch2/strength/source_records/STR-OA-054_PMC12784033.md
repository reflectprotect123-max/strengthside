**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
The paired t-test results indicated that both groups showed significant improvements in deadlift, back squat, push-ups, countermovement jump (CMJ), and sit-ups. In particular, CMJ, which represents a volleyball-specific fitness component, showed significant between-group differences and group × time interaction effects in the landmine group.
