**Evidence note (quoted/paraphrased from the fetched page):**
Describes "VESP," an autoregulation method using two signals: how heavy a weight feels at the start of a lift, and how fast the lift moved on video review afterward. The author's own stated rationale is that this generalizes better than RIR/RPE alone across bodybuilding's mix of explosive and controlled movements at both heavy and light loads. The author explicitly frames this as personal experimentation ("I'd love to hear your thoughts... I'll be experimenting with it a lot"), not an established or validated method.
