**Evidence note (quoted/paraphrased from the fetched page):**
TSS (Training Stress Score) scores one workout from duration and intensity relative to threshold (pace/HR/power); one hour at threshold = 100 TSS. CTL (Chronic Training Load, "Fitness") is a 42-day rolling average of daily TSS. ATL (Acute Training Load, "Fatigue") is a 7-day rolling average of TSS. TSB (Training Stress Balance, "Form") = CTL minus ATL, with the page's own stated ranges: +15 to +25 as a "peak performance" zone, -10 to -30 as an "optimal training" zone, and beyond -30 as needing recovery.

**Review state:** Research staging only. Not an executable rule, policy, model parameter, or athlete decision. The specific numeric TSB bands above are TrainingPeaks' own marketed guidance, not independently validated thresholds - flagged explicitly so a future reviewer does not mistake vendor guidance for verified science; this is exactly the kind of number this project's own governance forbids adopting without independent review (see docs/phase3-strength-session-gate-research-brief.md for the same boundary applied to strength).
