**Evidence note (from the article's own abstract, Results/Conclusions section, trimmed):**
A total of 17 RCTs (386 participants) were included. The analysis revealed that RST significantly improved short-sprint ability (g = -0.26, 95% CI [-0.50, -0.03], p = 0.03), RSA (g = -0.37, 95% CI [-0.67, -0.08], p = 0.02), change of direction (CoD) ability (g = -0.70, 95% CI [-1.14, -0.25], p 2 ≤ 25.7%), with only RSA and VJ performance showing low-to-moderate heterogeneity.
