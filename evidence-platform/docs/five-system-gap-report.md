# Five-system research gap report

All entries are corpus assessments, not claims of scientific validity.

## Strength

- **Have:** Explicit claim and formula registries; test vectors; exercise metadata; research syntheses
- **Partial:** Several PubMed-linked claims and transparent candidate formulas
- **Missing:** Independent source verification; population/method extraction; calibration and prospective validation
- **Proprietary/unrecoverable:** Private competitor estimators and coefficients
- **Primary-source research:** Load progression, RIR accuracy, volume-response, deload, concurrent training, safety boundaries

## Conditioning

- **Have:** Modality progression material; Concept2/Echo data contracts; source registries; integration notes
- **Partial:** Device/API evidence and progression trees are mixed with implementation packs
- **Missing:** Canonical conditioning metric dictionary; dose-response observations; modality-specific validation
- **Proprietary/unrecoverable:** Vendor algorithms, device firmware behavior, proprietary scoring
- **Primary-source research:** Intensity distribution, progression/regression, device validity, hybrid interference by modality/population

## Nutrition

- **Have:** Formula and claim registries; PubMed-linked review; AUSNUT/OFF provenance notes; tests and prototypes
- **Partial:** Transparent equations and logging-state rules, many marked inference/prototype
- **Missing:** Equation population bounds; uncertainty calibration; athlete-specific prospective validation; clinical safety ownership
- **Proprietary/unrecoverable:** MacroFactor production algorithm and coefficients
- **Primary-source research:** Energy expenditure estimation, weight-trend filters, protein/energy availability, missing/partial logging semantics

## Recovery

- **Have:** Five large AI-authored handoff/audit/synthesis documents
- **Partial:** Broad topic coverage but weak structured provenance and no canonical registry
- **Missing:** Primary studies, measurement protocols, reliability, clinical boundaries, structured observations and rules
- **Proprietary/unrecoverable:** Wearable vendor scores/coefficients and individual baselines not in archive
- **Primary-source research:** HRV/HR/sleep validity, illness/pain pathways, return-to-training, wearable missingness and uncertainty

## Coordinator

- **Have:** Three coordinator/onboarding/model-spec documents plus cross-system contracts
- **Partial:** Architecture concepts and candidate boundaries
- **Missing:** Owned arbitration policies, exact interface versions, feasible-set definition, objective functions and validation data
- **Proprietary/unrecoverable:** Production priorities, proprietary optimization weights, real athlete conflict data
- **Primary-source research:** Concurrent-training interaction, safety precedence, multi-objective optimization, abstention and replay
