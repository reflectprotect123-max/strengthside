# When research becomes required

Research begins at **promotion**, not collection or software plumbing.

The platform can ingest, hash, index, search, link, queue, validate structure, abstain and replay synthetic decisions without making a scientific claim. It cannot honestly decide what an athlete should do until evidence establishes the relevant relationship and its applicability.

## First unavoidable research action

Select one narrow decision path, retrieve its primary sources, verify every claim against the source, record population and measurement context, resolve contradictions, and obtain domain review. Only then may engineering define a tested rule contract or model candidate.

## Research is mandatory before

1. Marking a scientific or product-behaviour claim verified.
2. Choosing thresholds, coefficients, decay rates, confidence bands or objective weights.
3. Deciding whether evidence applies to this athlete and context.
4. Activating a Strength, Conditioning, Nutrition, Recovery or Coordinator rule.
5. Fitting, calibrating or activating a personal model.
6. Defining safety escalation or health-related policy.
7. Allowing a recommendation to be silently applied.

## Gate sequence

`candidate -> source verified -> structured extraction verified -> domain reviewed -> conflict resolved -> contract and tests complete -> shadow model -> personal calibration -> bounded activation`

The current archive stops before `source verified`. The `research-gate` command reports the exact queue and blockers.
