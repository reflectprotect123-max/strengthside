# Design and research scopes

These documents define the next program of work after the pre-research platform. They do not activate a model or promote scientific claims.

- `NON-LLM-AI-DESIGN-SCOPE.md`: control-system and model architecture.
- `RESEARCH-DEPTH-SCOPE.md`: evidence programme and activation standards.
- `AI-RESEARCH-CROSSWALK.md`: reconciled build/research stages and scenario scale.

## Fixed architecture

- Product: **THE Hybrid System**.
- Five systems: Strength, Conditioning, Nutrition, Recovery and Coordinator.
- All five publish versioned outputs to the **Multi-model adaptive control system**.
- That higher layer performs state estimation, personal adaptation and constrained decision optimization.
- Runtime is non-LLM, deterministic, bounded, versioned and replayable.
- Gemini or another LLM may help with offline extraction only; all output is untrusted until reviewed.

## Scope rule

AI design may specify interfaces, model families, lifecycle and tests. Research scope may specify questions, protocols and evidence gates. Neither document may invent scientific coefficients, thresholds or claim that the current engines are validated.
